## Rulings (owner decisions, numbered, carried across revisions)
R1–R169 carry as last written, except R82 (withdrawn) and R145, R146 (superseded).
R170 **Tutors from anywhere; permit optional (owner, 2026-10-01).** Tutors may be in the UAE, Pakistan, India or anywhere else; this is how price variation arises within the bands. The UAE tutoring permit is **optional for everyone**: `permit_number`, `permit_expires_at` and every document type become optional at onboarding and for approval. The brief's vetting claim changes from "every tutor holds a UAE permit" to "every tutor is approved by TrusTutor after a CV or LinkedIn review" — trade-off stated to the owner once and accepted. Onboarding asks for **country** and **timezone** (country new on `tutor_profiles`; timezone already exists). When country is UAE the onboarding shows a note that a tutoring permit is required by UAE law and offers the permit fields; it never blocks. **Bookable** becomes `status = approved AND (permit_expires_at IS NULL OR permit_expires_at > today)` — the scope `bookable()` and `permitIsValid()` change together; the permit-expiry reminder and the "expiring permits" dashboard widget apply only to tutors with a permit. ADR-024 records the change and supersedes the brief's "UAE permit forces vetting" line. Phase 2 (owner): decide what becomes mandatory to be searchable; nothing more is made mandatory now.
R171 **CV or LinkedIn required; most other fields optional (owner, 2026-10-01).** Onboarding requires **one** of: a CV upload (new document type `cv`, PDF/DOC/DOCX/JPG/PNG, 10 MB) or a LinkedIn profile URL (new `linkedin_url` on `tutor_profiles`, validated as a linkedin.com URL). Submission for review requires only: name, country, timezone, that CV-or-LinkedIn, and the tutor agreement acceptance. Headline, bio, intro video, permit, other documents and bank details stay optional at submission. **Minimum to be approved and searchable** (enforced by `ApproveTutor`, not by onboarding): at least one subject with curriculum and year range, an hourly rate inside the band, and at least one availability window — the admin's review page shows which of the three are missing and lets the admin **edit headline, bio, subjects and rate** on the tutor's behalf from the CV (new admin edit actions, audited) before approving. `document_types.required` rows are set to false by migration; the admin can flip them later. CV parsing or any automatic field extraction is **not built** (v1 non-goal: AI); it is on the roadmap after soft launch.
R172 **Counsel note (owner, 2026-10-01).** A UAE platform paying tutors overseas has tax, payout-rail and contract implications; the planner adds it to the D-04 counsel list in PROJECT_BRIEF.md at the next refresh. Nothing to build.

(b) The onboarding permit step asked for permit number and expiry as required fields with no skip — superseded by R170; the step becomes optional with a visible "Skip for now".
