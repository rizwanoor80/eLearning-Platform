Tutor balance buckets are derived, never stored.
R53 Money rules, absolute. Every amount is integer fils through `App\\Support\\Money` — no float, no `round()` on money, no arithmetic on a raw money integer outside the value object; a test greps the diff areas for float-typed money. `TutorProfile::trialPrice()` is the ONE place a trial price is computed; the onboarding preview's `100 − pct` rounding is deleted and replaced by a call to it, with a test proving preview and booked price agree across the whole band table including an odd-fils case. Frozen onto the lesson at creation and never re-read from settings: `price`, `commission_pct`, `commission_amount`, `tutor_amount`, `cancel_window_hours`, `student_grace_min`, `tutor_grace_min`. A test changes all seven settings after booking and asserts the outcome is unchanged (CP3 box 5). `commission_amount + tutor_amount = price` exactly, remainder fil to the platform, tested at a price that does not divide evenly.
R54 Deletion decided (the open CP2 question). A learner is soft-deleted only when they have no lesson in a non-terminal state; the attempt is refused with a message naming the blocking lessons. A user is never hard-deleted: deletion anonymises (name, email, phone replaced with a tombstone, credentials cleared, sessions and tokens revoked) and leaves `lessons`, `payments` and `ledger_entries` intact with their foreign keys, because financial history must outlive the person. Soft-deleted learners stay visible on past lessons. Admin action, audited; not exposed to parents in CP3.
