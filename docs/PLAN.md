## Rulings (owner decisions, numbered, carried across revisions)
R1–R244 carry as last written, except R82 (withdrawn), R145, R146, R236 (superseded).
R245 **CP5 architecture: collection and payouts are separate (owner, 2026-10-20).** Collection: `PaymentGateway` charges, saves cards and refunds; settlement is to the company's UAE bank account; the ledger's `gateway` account is that money. Payouts: never through the collection gateway; a tutor is paid from the company's funds by a **payout method** (new concept, R249) — the method is the tutor's choice among those the admin has enabled for the tutor's `tutor_profiles.country`. In v1 every payout method is **manual**: TrusTutor computes who is owed what, groups it by method, exports a file per method, the admin pays in the bank's or provider's own portal, and records each payment's external reference. API-driven methods (Payoneer, PayPal, Stripe, a bank API) are later drivers behind the same registry, each its own plan. CC records R245 as ADR-030.
R246 **23a — payment gateway registry (CP5 task; invariant 16).** New `payment_gateways` table and Filament resource exactly as CP5 lists: code, name, mode (`test`/`live`), credentials as encrypted JSON entered field by field and shown masked, `supports_saved_cards`, `is_active` with a one-active partial unique index. `PaymentGatewayServiceProvider` resolves the driver from the active row; the only driver today is `fake` (the existing `FakePaymentGateway`), which cannot be activated when `APP_ENV=production` (keep `fakeGatewayAllowed()` as the guard). With no active row, booking shows a (new) admin-notice page and creates no `payments` row. Proof: the CP5 acceptance lines on the registry, credentials never in logs or serialisation (18a pattern).
R247 **23b — saved cards for parents (CP5 task; invariant 15).** The parent portal's card page becomes driver-neutral: list saved cards (brand, last four, expiry — nothing else), add or replace through the active driver's `saveCard`, remove, and an "expired or expiring" notice; the fake driver's existing test-card form stays the fake driver's add step (`TestCardController`, R100/R107). No real card capture UI is built until the Stripe driver.
R248 **23c — parent receipts (CP5 task).** A receipts page listing each paid lesson: date, tutor display name, learner, amount in AED, the payment reference, and any refund lines; one printable receipt per lesson. **No VAT line** until D-04 says whether and how VAT applies; the page says nothing about VAT. No email receipt yet (it follows Postmark).
R249 **23d — payout methods and tutor preference (new).** New admin registry `payout_methods`: code (`bank_transfer`, `payoneer`, `paypal`, `stripe`, `wise` — an enum), name, `is_active`, `countries` (ISO-3166 alpha-2 list; empty = all), and the detail fields it needs from a tutor (bank: the existing `bank_*` fields on `tutor_profiles`; Payoneer / PayPal / Wise / Stripe: the account email). Seeded: `bank_transfer` active for all countries; the others inactive until the admin enables them with countries. A tutor picks a preferred method in Settings (only methods active for their country are offered) and enters its details; the details are encrypted at rest, hidden from serialisation like the bank fields (18a), and shown to admins masked except on the payout export. A tutor with no valid method is listed to admins as "cannot be paid yet" and never blocks anything else.
R250 **23e — tutor earnings with the five buckets (CP5 task; CLAUDE.md "never a single stored number").** The tutor's earnings page computes pending, on hold, available, processing and paid to date from lessons and the ledger, with the definitions written in the page's docblock (pending: confirmed or completed, not yet released; on hold: disputed; available: released to the tutor, not in a batch; processing: in an unpaid batch line; paid to date: in a paid batch line). Until 23f merges, processing and paid to date are 0 and the payout history is empty. Proof: one test per bucket transition, and the sum of the five equals the tutor's lifetime earnings from the ledger.
R251 **23f — payout batches, one file per method (CP5 task, extended by R245).** Admin generates a batch for a period (default cadence `payout_weekday`); it includes only tutors whose available balance is at least `payout_min` (20000 fils by default) and who have a valid method; lines are grouped by method; each method exports its own CSV (bank: account name, IBAN, SWIFT, amount, currency, reference; email methods: email, amount, currency, reference); each line is marked paid only with an external reference (required), or marked failed (back to available). Marking paid writes the `payout` ledger entries (R253) and moves the tutor's money from processing to paid to date. Proof: the CP5 acceptance line on payout batches, a batch with two methods, a failed line, `ledger:verify` green.
R252 **Rehearsal migrations are authorised for this cycle (rule 8, named).** The additive migrations of 23a, 23d and 23f may run on trustutor-rehearsal through the push-to-deploy under R111/R228; the R213 check must show `pending 0` after each deploy. Production is untouched.
