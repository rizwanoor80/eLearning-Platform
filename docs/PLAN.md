R133 **Messaging.** A conversation exists only between an account holder (parent or adult student) and a tutor profile, created idempotently (unique pair) when a lesson between them becomes `confirmed` or is created `reserved`; an expired `pending_payment` creates none; a conversation outlives cancellations. Messages: plain text, max 2000 characters, never edited or deleted (safeguarding), 30 per minute per user throttle; `read_at` set when the recipient opens the conversation. R134 **Masking — store masked only.** Before `first_lesson_completed_at`, emails, phone numbers (runs of 7+ digits allowing spaces, dots, dashes, brackets and a leading `+`; Arabic-Indic digits count as digits) and URLs (with or without scheme, including bare domains such as `name.com`) are replaced by "[hidden until after your first lesson]" before the row is written; the original is never stored anywhere (row, log, job payload, event, email), and `body_masked=true`. Messages sent before that date stay masked afterwards. The masker is one pure class with a fixture table of cases in its tests; spelled-out numbers ("zero five zero…") are a disclosed known limit, not built. Recorded as an ADR.
R135 **Unread badges by polling; Reverb deferred to CP8.** The unread message and notification counts come from one small JSON endpoint polled every 60 s while the tab is visible.
